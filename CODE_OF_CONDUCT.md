# Community Code of Conduct

The quirq community welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. Help make it a place where people can ask questions, share work, and disagree productively.

## How we work together

- Treat people with respect. Discuss the work and its effects without attacking the person.
- Give useful context, acknowledge uncertainty, and accept corrections.
- Make room for newcomers and people whose experience differs from yours.
- Respect privacy and boundaries. Ask before sharing someone else's private messages or personal information.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual harassment, deliberate intimidation, and repeated unwanted contact are not acceptable. Neither are publishing private information without consent, targeted disruption, or spam.

This standard applies in quirq's repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community spaces, and when someone represents the project publicly.

## Report a concern

Email [team@xo.builders](mailto:team@xo.builders?subject=Community%20conduct%20concern) with the subject **Community conduct concern**. Include the relevant links or context and any concerns about your privacy. Do not post sensitive reports publicly.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, ask someone to change their behavior, limit participation, or remove access depending on the severity and pattern of the conduct. People handling a report should avoid participating in decisions where they have a conflict of interest. Concerns about a moderation decision can be sent to the same contact with additional context.
